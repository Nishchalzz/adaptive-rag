# Research Laboratories at the Halvorsen Institute of Technology

> Synthetic demonstration document. The laboratories, people and figures described here are fictional.

## Retrieval and Reasoning Lab

The Retrieval and Reasoning Lab (RRL) belongs to the School of Computing and is located on the third
floor of Aster Hall. It was founded in 2016 and is led by Dr. Lena Park. The lab has 14 members: three
faculty members, eight doctoral students and three research engineers. In 2024 its research funding was
2.4 million euros, about 70 percent of which came from the Nordic Research Council.

The lab runs two main projects. Project Veritas, started in 2022, develops methods that check whether
statements generated by language models are supported by source documents. Project Atlas, started in
2023, studies multi-hop retrieval for questions that need information from several documents.

## Grid Storage Lab

The Grid Storage Lab is part of the School of Energy Systems and is located in Birch Pavilion. It is led
by Prof. Anika Bergström, who is also dean of the school. The lab develops sodium-ion batteries for
storing renewable energy. In 2023 it commissioned a pilot storage plant with a capacity of 4
megawatt-hours on the eastern edge of the campus. The plant is operated together with the city utility
Lindmark Energi.

## Neural Interfaces Lab

The Neural Interfaces Lab belongs to the School of Biomedical Engineering and is located in Cedar Centre.
It is led by Dr. Kofi Mensah and was founded in 2018. The lab builds low-power sensors that record nerve
signals for prosthetic limbs. Since 2024 it has collaborated with the Retrieval and Reasoning Lab on
Project Veritas to summarise clinical notes with verified citations.

## Shared facilities

The institute's high-performance computing cluster, called Nordlys, is located in the basement of Aster
Hall and has 96 GPU nodes. All three laboratories book time on Nordlys through the research office.
