## History

The Halvorsen Institute of Technology (HIT) was founded in 1987 in the city of Lindmark by the civil
engineer Eva Halvorsen, who also served as its first president until 1999. The institute began with
two schools and 640 students. In 2005 it opened its third school, the School of Biomedical Engineering.

## Leadership

Dr. Mira Okafor has been president of the institute since 2019. Before becoming president she was dean
of the School of Computing from 2011 to 2019.

## Schools

- School of Computing: dean Prof. Tomas Reyes since 2019. The school is based in Aster Hall.
- School of Energy Systems: dean Prof. Anika Bergström since 2016. The school is based in Birch Pavilion.
- School of Biomedical Engineering: dean Prof. Samuel Adeyemi since 2021. The school is based in Cedar Centre.

## Campus

The main campus covers 42 hectares on the northern bank of the river Vell. Aster Hall, the largest
building, was completed in 2003 and has six floors. Birch Pavilion was completed in 1991. Cedar Centre
opened together with the School of Biomedical Engineering in 2005.

The Fenwick Library opened in 1994. It holds about 310,000 volumes and is open 24 hours a day during
examination periods.

## Students and staff

In the 2024 academic year the institute had 9,400 students, of whom 38 percent were graduate students,
and 1,150 academic staff. The School of Computing is the largest school, with 4,100 students.
